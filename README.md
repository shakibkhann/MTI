# Money Trace Investigation (MTI)

## 🛡️ About

Money Trace Investigation (MTI) is an India-focused online fraud investigation assistance platform.

MTI helps fraud victims organize their case information, understand available evidence, and prepare information for reporting to the appropriate authorities.

## 🚀 Features

- Fraud case submission
- Digital evidence organization
- Basic OSINT-based investigation assistance
- Fraud case guidance
- ₹100 consultation service
- Investigation report assistance
- Google Form based case intake
- Simple and secure user workflow

## 💰 Pricing

- Case submission: Free
- Investigation assistance: Free
- Investigation report: Free
- Consultation: ₹100 / 30 minutes
- Recovery fee: ₹0

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Visit the MTI website
2. Submit your fraud case details
3. Provide relevant transaction/evidence information
4. Pay ₹100 if consultation is required
5. Payment is verified
6. Consultation and investigation guidance is provided

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

MTI does not ask users to share:

- OTP
- UPI PIN
- ATM PIN
- CVV
- Internet banking password
- Email passwords

Users should only provide information that is necessary for investigating and reporting their case.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

MTI is an independent fraud investigation assistance service.

MTI is not a government organization, police department, bank, or law-enforcement agency.

MTI does not guarantee recovery of lost money.

Actual freezing, blocking, investigation, seizure, or recovery of funds is handled by banks, payment providers, police/cybercrime authorities, and courts as applicable.
